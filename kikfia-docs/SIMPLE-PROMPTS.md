# Kikfia Simple Prompts (portable houses)

Short, simple prompts that show the **product**: portable houses that are delivered, placed and lived in. They aim for natural light and a real-photo look, simple and clean, with no luxury real-estate style.

**Every prompt already ends with:** realistic photo, no people's faces, no text, no logos.

**The look of every house** (matching your video and the photo you liked): a single-story box shape, natural cedar wood in the middle, light gray panels on the sides, black window frames and a big glass sliding door.

**Settings:**
- **Images:** 16:9 for wide pictures, 4:3 for cards, 4:5 for interiors. Highest quality.
- **Videos:** image-to-video from the matching image, 5 to 6 seconds, 1080p, 16:9, audio off.
