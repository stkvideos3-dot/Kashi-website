# KIKFIA website: image and video prompts

Make these with any image and video tool you like, then drag the files into the chat.

## Rules for every file

- **Landscape 16:9** for the starting image, the video, and the interior and delivery stills. **4:3** for the three use-case cards.
- **No text, no logos, no signs, no watermarks** anywhere in the picture. If your tool adds any, make it again.
- **Keep the house the same in every shot:** a compact single-story modern home with matte charcoal steel cladding, warm cedar wood accents, a wide wall of glass doors, and a small wooden deck.
- **Using your real house?** If you have a photo of your actual KIKFIA model, attach it as a reference image for every prompt. Then change the house description in each prompt to match your model.
- **Sending the files:** send the original files at full quality. Apps like WhatsApp squash videos, so avoid them for this.

## 1. The starting image (frame one of the video)

```
High aerial drone view at golden hour, looking down and forward over a quiet
green valley, composed as the first moment of a slow drone descent toward one
small modern home. Far below, in the right half of the frame, a compact
single-story modern portable house sits alone on a gentle meadow plot: a clean
rectangular home with matte charcoal steel cladding, warm cedar wood accents,
a wide wall of glass doors and a small wooden deck, its interior lights
glowing softly. Thin wisps of low mist drift between the camera and the
valley. Low warm sun from the left, long soft shadows, honey-gold light on
the grass, a pale peach and soft blue haze along the horizon. Rolling meadow,
scattered pine trees and distant hills continue edge to edge across the whole
frame, with calm open sky across the top and soft sunlit meadow filling the
left side of the frame. Cinematic, photorealistic, natural color, 16:9.
No text, no logos, no lettering anywhere.
```

## 2. The hero video (image-to-video, made from image 1)

**Settings:** use image 1 as the start frame (image-to-video). 1080p, landscape 16:9, **6 to 8 seconds**, **no audio or music**, standard or pro quality, no camera preset or effect template.

```
One continuous shot, no cuts. The camera descends slowly and smoothly from
high above the valley toward the small modern house, sinking straight down
while gently tilting up from a downward view to eye level. The house stays in
the same spot in the right half of the frame the whole time and grows steadily
larger. Early in the shot the camera sinks through a thin veil of low mist,
with soft moisture beading on the lens and one beat of gentle blur before the
view clears. The scene stays alive throughout: tall grass sways in a light
breeze, the mist drifts and thins, warm sunlight glints through the pine
trees, a few birds glide far in the distance. The shot ends at rest: the
camera settles at eye level about fifteen meters in front of the house, which
sits right of center on its meadow plot, fully in frame with generous space
above the roof and below the deck, warm light glowing through its wide glass
doors, golden evening sun across the grass, and open meadow and soft sky
filling the left side of the frame. The camera comes to a complete stop and
holds perfectly still for the final second. No text, no logos, no lettering
anywhere.
```

**Before you send it, check:** the camera only moves down and forward, with no jumps or cuts. The house keeps its shape and does not melt or change. The last second is calm and still.
