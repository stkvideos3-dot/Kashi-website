## How to make AI images look real, not like renders

Add these habits to every prompt. They are the difference between "AI render" and "real photo":

- **Write it like a photographer.** Add: `shot on a full-frame camera, 35mm lens, f/2.8, natural light, candid documentary photography`.
- **People in the middle of doing something,** not posing and not looking at the camera.
- **Keep faces small or turned away.** Close-up faces are the fastest giveaway that an image is AI.
- **Add real-life clutter:** a coffee mug, a dog bed, a pair of shoes by the door, footprints in the grass.
- **Natural color.** Avoid glowing HDR skies, perfect symmetry, and words like "8K, ultra detailed, masterpiece".
- **Keep the same house in every shot:** a single-story modern cabin with a dark charcoal standing-seam metal gable roof, a dark charcoal box dormer, vertical natural wood siding, a light gray gable-end panel, a covered wooden porch, and tall black-framed glass sliding doors.
- **Real beats AI.** As soon as you have photos of real KIKFIA projects and customers (with their permission), send them and I'll swap them in.
- **Always end with:** `No text, no logos, no lettering anywhere.`
