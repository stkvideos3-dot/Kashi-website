Every image and video spot on the site, and what fills it now. Send a file with the filename shown and it drops straight in.

**Balance:** about half lifestyle (people are the hero, the structure supports the story) and half structures.

| File | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `hero-scrub.mp4` | The family house film: land, the house arrives, interior, kitchen, family, final wide | One file, 10 s, scrubbed by scroll. Encoded with a keyframe every 8 frames so scrubbing stays smooth |

The captions use dark text with a soft light glow, because this film is bright. They sit in the sky or on light surfaces, never over a face or the house.

## When you have real customers

Real reviews and case studies can come back later. For each one, send:

- **Testimonial:** the customer's exact words, first name and last initial, city and state, project type, and their written permission (a photo is optional).
- **Review rating:** a link to your Google or Facebook reviews page. I show the real rating and count.
- **Case study:** goal, location, size, timeline, then problem, solution, and result in one or two sentences each, plus photos.
