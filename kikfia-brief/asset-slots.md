**Balance:** about half lifestyle (people are the hero, the structure supports the story) and half structures.

## Hero (scroll film)

| File | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `hero-scrub.mp4` | The family house film: land, the house arrives, interior, kitchen, family, final wide | One file, 10 s, scrubbed by scroll. Encoded with a keyframe every 8 frames so scrubbing stays smooth |
| `hero-poster.jpg` | First frame (empty land) | Shown while the film loads |
| `hero-ending.jpg` | Last frame (the house on the hill) | Phone hero background and the faded consultation background |

The captions follow the film:

| Scroll | Shot | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 13% | Empty land | You have the land. Now you need the space. |
| 14 to 40% | The house arrives, then the living room | No caption. The house is the answer |
| 41 to 51% | Kitchen | Designed around how you'll live, work, or earn. |
| 52 to 61% | Mom with the kids | No caption |
| 61 to 71% | Dad lifting his son | Space for the people you love. |
| 72 to 82% | Dad and son close-up | No caption |
| 83 to 100% | Final wide | Custom Portable Spaces Built Around Your Life, plus the two buttons |

The captions use dark text with a soft light glow, because this film is bright. They sit in the sky or on light surfaces, never over a face or the house.

## Social proof (real content only)

Project stories and client reviews stay hidden on the live site until real content replaces the placeholders. The dramatized films are not used there, because that section is for real customers. For each real item, send:

- **Testimonial:** the customer's exact words, first name and last initial, city and state, project type, and their written permission (a photo is optional).
- **Review rating:** a link to your Google or Facebook reviews page. I show the real rating and count.
- **Case study:** goal, location, size, timeline, then problem, solution, and result in one or two sentences each, plus photos.
- **Before and after:** two photos from the same spot and angle.
