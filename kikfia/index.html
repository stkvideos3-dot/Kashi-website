    Questions

    The questions people ask first.

    Straight answers. If yours isn't here, ask it in your free consultation.

    I don't know which structure is right for me. Can KIKFIA help?

    Yes. That's what the free consultation is for. Tell us what you need the space to do, where it's going, and your budget, and we'll recommend the right type, size, and layout, and explain why.

    Can you create a completely custom design?

    Yes. Size, layout, rooms, kitchen, windows, roofing, finishes, and smart features are all yours to choose. We turn your ideas into drawings you approve before anything is built.

    Can I use my structure as an Airbnb rental?

    Yes, as long as your city and HOA allow short-term rentals. We help you check the rules early, then design the unit for guests: a private entrance, a full bathroom, and finishes that look great in listing photos.

    Can you help me maximize space on my property?

    Yes. Send us your lot size or a few photos of the area. We'll suggest a footprint and layout that fits your setbacks and still leaves room for a deck, a garden, or parking.

    What if I have never bought a portable structure before?

    Then we walk you through every step in plain language: what to prepare on your land, which permits to ask about, and what happens on delivery day. You always know what comes next.

    How does the process work, from consultation to installation?

    Four steps: a free consultation, a custom design you approve, the build, then delivery and installation on your property. You get one point of contact and a written timeline at the start.

    Can you help me choose the best option for my budget and goals?

    Yes. Share your budget range in the consultation and we'll show you what it can include, with options to add or remove, so you stay in control of the final price.

    What types of properties are suitable?

    Most properties with a level spot and room for a delivery truck: suburban backyards, rural land, lakefront lots, farms, and commercial sites. If your land slopes or access is tight, we plan around it.

    Do I need a permit?

    Usually, yes, especially for a space someone lives in. The rules change from city to city. We prepare the floor plans and documents your permit office asks for, plus a short list of questions to ask them before you order.
