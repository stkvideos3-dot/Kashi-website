06 · Questions

The questions buyers ask first.

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, ask it in your quote request.

Can I trust a company overseas with this much money?

It's the right question to ask. KIKFIA only works with factories we have checked: business licence verified and a live video walk-through of the factory floor. You pay into a company bank account, never a personal one, and you see your home in photos and video before it ships.

What will it really cost, all-in?

Your quote puts the house, ocean freight, import duties, customs, and trucking to your lot into one price. The only costs outside it are local ones, like your pad, hookups, and permit fees, and we help you price those before you order.

Do I need a permit?

Usually, yes, especially for a backyard home someone lives in. The rules change from city to city. We send the floor plans and documents your permit office asks for, plus a short list of questions to ask them before you order.

How long does it take?

Plan on roughly two to three months from order to delivery: about a month to build, about a month at sea, then customs and trucking. Your quote gives you your own dates.

What do I need to get ready on my land?

A level gravel pad or simple foundation, room for a truck to reach the spot, and power and water nearby. We send the exact specs, and we confirm your delivery date well ahead, so you are never rushing to get ready the night before.

Will it handle heat and cold?

The walls and roof are insulated panels, and the windows are double glazed. Send us your ZIP code and we match the insulation to your climate.

Can I see my home before it ships?

Yes. You get photos and a video of your finished home at the factory before it is loaded.
